How to use Grade Calculator

Test score divides earned points by possible points. Weighted grade multiplies each category’s percentage by its share of the course. Final exam mode solves for the exam score needed to reach your target, assuming your current grade represents all non-final work.

Can a required final score exceed 100%?

Yes. That means the target is not reachable under the entered weights without extra credit or a grading-policy change. A negative required score means the target is already met even with zero on the final.
