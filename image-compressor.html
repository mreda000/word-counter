How to use Image Compressor

Select an image, enter a maximum file size in KB, and choose JPEG or WebP. The tool adjusts quality and, when needed, dimensions to meet your target. It never upscales an image. Download the result when you are happy with the preview.

Are my photos uploaded?

No. Image decoding and compression happen in your browser. The tool does not send your image to ChickenTask or an image-processing service. Normal site services and ads may still make their own network requests.
