About the project

About ChickenTask

ChickenTask brings small, useful tasks and short practice breaks into one website. You can turn a screenshot into editable text, organize PDF pages, prepare a piece of writing, or take a break with a browser game. The tools and games are free to use without creating an account.

From a word counter to an everyday toolbox

The project started with a word counter and text analyzer. It now includes tools for documents, images, calculations and group activities, alongside typing practice, word puzzles, aim practice and Pet the Chicken. The original Word Counter is still available as its own tool.

The connection between these tools is practical: extract text from an image, check the recognition result, then send it to the word counter; or combine PDF files and review their page order before sharing the result. Our step-by-step guide explains these workflows and their limits.

Who maintains ChickenTask?

ChickenTask is maintained by Moe. The website's source and change history are available in the ChickenTask GitHub repository. Suggestions, correction requests and reports of broken tools can be sent to countmywords.com@gmail.com.

How to use the results responsibly

  • Writing estimates are approximate. Reading and speaking times use fixed rates; the English reading-grade estimate uses a simple syllable approximation. Your own rehearsal and judgment matter more than a single score.
  • Review converted files and text. Image recognition can confuse characters, and a newly combined PDF may not preserve interactive forms or digital signatures. Keep the original files.
  • Practice scores describe a browser session. Input devices, browser behavior and hardware affect game results. Aim Trainer is general practice, not an exact simulation of another game's controls.

Privacy and optional accounts

The image, PDF and text tools process the content you select or type in your browser. Fetching text from a URL sends a request to that website. Optional accounts can synchronize supported game records; they do not synchronize your documents or focus-task list.

Hosting, advertising, sign-in and other site services can make their own network requests. Optional analytics is off until you choose to allow it. See the Privacy Policy for the details and use Privacy choices in the footer to manage the available options.

How we present guides and corrections

Our guides use examples tied to the tools on this site, explain calculation assumptions, and identify relevant limitations. A guide's updated date records a content revision. It is not a promise that every browser behaves identically or that an external service will accept a result.

If something looks wrong, send the page address, what you expected, what happened, and your browser or device. A small non-sensitive example is helpful; please do not email passwords or private documents.
