What’s on your wheel?

Up to 60 entries. Repeated entries get an extra chance. Shared links include the names you enter, so share only what you intend.

How to use Spin the Wheel

Enter names, food options, classroom activities, or game challenges—one on each line. Each entry gets the same chance. Remove the winner to make the next pick without replacement. Saved wheels stay in this browser, and share links work without an account.

Is the wheel random?

The winner is chosen using your browser’s cryptographic random-number generator. The animation then lands on that selected entry. This is a casual decision tool, not a certified drawing service.
