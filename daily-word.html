Daily Word Puzzle

Play a free daily five-letter word puzzle. Use color clues to find today's word in six guesses, with progress saved in your browser.

How to play Daily Word Puzzle

Enter a five-letter guess. Green marks the correct letter and position; gold means the letter belongs elsewhere. You have six guesses. A new puzzle arrives each day at midnight UTC.

Read the clues before your next guess

Each row gives feedback for a five-letter guess. Green means the letter is in the correct position. Gold means the letter occurs in the answer but belongs in another available position. Gray means that copy of the letter has not matched. You have six different guesses; entering the same guess twice does not use another row.

Repeated letters: a worked example

Suppose the answer is APPLE and you guess ALLEY. A is green. One L is gold because the answer has an L in another position; the second L is gray because that single L has already been accounted for. E is gold and Y is gray. A gray duplicate does not always mean the letter is absent from the answer.

Build a more informative guess

  1. Begin with a five-letter combination that tests several different letters.
  2. Keep confirmed green positions when useful and try gold letters in new positions.
  3. Use gray clues together with duplicate-letter counts, rather than ruling out every gray letter automatically.
  4. Near the end, compare the remaining possibilities and choose a guess that tests the uncertain positions.

When the puzzle changes

The daily answer is selected using the UTC date, so midnight UTC may occur in the afternoon or evening where you live. Reload after the date changes to open the new puzzle. This is a small curated answer collection and answers can repeat over time. The input accepts any five A–Z letters; it does not check guesses against a complete English dictionary.

Guesses are saved locally for that day's puzzle. Refreshing normally restores them, but clearing browser storage can erase progress. After six guesses the answer is shown. For a replayable timed challenge, try Word Scramble.
