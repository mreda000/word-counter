How to play

Choose your time and difficulty. Start typing the displayed passage. Green letters are correct and red letters need attention. Your speed is calculated from correctly typed characters. No sign-up needed; your personal best is saved only in this browser.

Practice typing with a consistent comparison

Choose a duration and difficulty, then type the displayed passage into the input box. The timer begins with your first input. Easy passages emphasize everyday words; harder passages add punctuation, numbers and less familiar character combinations. Changing duration or difficulty starts a fresh test.

How WPM and accuracy are calculated

This test treats five correct characters as one word. WPM equals correct characters divided by five, divided by elapsed minutes. For example, 250 correct characters in one minute gives 50 WPM. Accuracy is the percentage of characters currently in your input that match the passage at the same position. It is not a lifetime count of every key you pressed: correcting an error changes the current result.

A missed or extra character can shift later characters out of position. Use the highlighted passage to find the first mismatch rather than typing faster through it. The live WPM estimate can jump during the opening seconds because very little time has elapsed.

A useful practice routine

  1. Use the same keyboard, duration and difficulty for several sessions.
  2. Finish one comfortable round and note both WPM and accuracy.
  3. Repeat slowly enough to reduce your common mistakes, especially spaces and punctuation.
  4. Compare several completed rounds instead of treating one fast attempt as your usual speed.

Saved records and troubleshooting

Personal bests are grouped by difficulty and duration and are saved in this browser. Clearing site data or using another browser can remove or separate those records. If typing does not start, click the input box; after a completed round choose Play again or Restart. Avoid paste and autofill if you want a meaningful practice result. Keyboard layouts, phone suggestions and browser performance can change the experience.
