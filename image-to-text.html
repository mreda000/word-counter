How do I get better results?

Use a clear, upright image with dark lettering on a plain background. Crop out distractions first. This tool recognizes printed English text; handwriting and complex tables may not be accurate.

Does this work with PDF files?

This page accepts images. Take a screenshot of the page you need, then extract and review the text. It does not bypass document protections.

Normal site services may make network requests. Your selected files and extracted text are not uploaded by these tools.
