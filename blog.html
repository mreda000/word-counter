Skill tips and game guides

🐔 Find every chicken.

Take your time checking the barn, trees and far corners of the farm. On desktop use WASD and mouse; on mobile use the joystick and swipe controls. Play Pet the Chicken.

🎯 Warm up before a match.

Try the guided five-minute routine with 75 seconds each of flick shots, target switching, moving targets and tracking. Browser aim practice is not an exact Valorant or CS2 sensitivity match. Open Aim Trainer.

⌨️ Focus on accuracy first.

When practicing typing, try to reduce mistakes before pushing for a higher WPM. Short, repeatable practice sessions make it easier to track improvement. Test your typing.
