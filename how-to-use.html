How to Use ChickenTask

Choose the result you need, follow the steps, and check the output before you use it. Games and tools work without signing in; optional accounts are for supported game records.

Turn a screenshot into usable writing

  1. Open Image to Text and choose a clear JPG, PNG or WebP image. The current limits are 15 MB and 20 megapixels. Recognition is configured for printed English.
  2. Run text extraction, then compare the result with the image. Check names, numbers, punctuation and line breaks; recognition errors can change meaning.
  3. Edit the result and copy or download it. To check its length, send it to Word Counter. This transfer uses temporary browser storage rather than uploading the text to an account.
  4. Review the word count, character count and estimated times. For a speech, rehearse with a timer; for an assignment, follow the submission system's counting rules.

Already have editable text? Paste it directly into Word Counter. Its sample texts let you check how the counters behave before using your own writing. See how to interpret the results or work through a shorter edit.

If a webpage will not import: the source site may refuse browser requests or generate its article after loading. Copy the article text yourself and paste it into the editor. Never paste a private sign-in link or a URL containing a secret.

Combine PDFs in the right order

  1. Open PDF Merger & Page Organizer and add the files you want to combine. A batch supports up to 10 files, 50 MB in total and 100 pages.
  2. Read the source filename and page number under each preview. Move pages earlier or later, or drag them into order. Remove unwanted pages from the output.
  3. Download the combined PDF, open that downloaded file, and check the first page, last page and the transitions between documents.

For example, to put a one-page cover before a two-page report, add both documents, move the cover preview to the first position, and check that the output has three pages. Removing a preview does not delete a page from your original file.

Password-protected PDFs are not supported. The export is a new document: interactive forms, bookmarks and digital signatures may not survive or remain valid. Keep your originals and use the original document workflow when those features matter.

Prepare an image or a QR code

Meet an image upload limit

In Image Compressor, select your image, set a target file size and maximum width, and choose JPEG or WebP. Compare the preview and the reported output size before downloading. If the result still exceeds your destination's limit, reduce its dimensions or adjust the settings and try again. Transparent areas are flattened to white in this tool, so keep the original if you need transparency.

Share a link, text or Wi-Fi details

In QR Code Generator, choose the type of information, enter it, generate the code, and download PNG or SVG. Scan the downloaded image with another device before printing it. A static QR code contains the information you entered; changing a destination later requires a new code unless you control the destination URL. Anyone who scans a Wi-Fi code can read the network details, so share it only with the intended people.

Plan a session and check a calculation

Focus on one small task

Open Focus Timer, add a specific task, choose your focus and break durations, and apply them before starting. Mark completed tasks as you finish. Each next session starts when you choose. Keep the tab open; a suspended background tab may show completion only when you return. Durations and up to 20 tasks can be saved in this browser, not your account.

Work out a percentage

Choose the matching mode in Percentage & Discount Calculator before entering values. For example, 25% off 80 saves 20 and leaves a price of 60. A change from 80 to 60 is −25%, because the change is compared with the original value. The displayed calculation helps you check that you chose the correct base.

Estimate a grade

Use Grade Calculator for a test percentage, weighted grade or final-exam target. Enter the weights from your course rules. If coursework is 40% of the grade and an exam is 60%, scores of 80 and 90 produce 80 × 0.40 + 90 × 0.60 = 86. Check rounding, missing work and extra-credit rules with your course; the tool cannot infer them.

Pick an option or organize a group

Spin the Wheel chooses from the entries you provide. Review the list before spinning, especially repeated names. Saving a list keeps it in this browser; a share link includes the entries in the link, so do not include private information.

Random Team Generator splits your entered names into groups. Review the output for duplicates and any real-world constraints before using it. Random grouping does not guarantee equal skill levels.

Gaming Name Generator offers name ideas and saves favorites in this browser. A generated suggestion is not a check of username availability, trademarks or permission to use another person's identity. Check your chosen platform before adopting it.

Take a practice break

Pet the Chicken

Explore the farm and pet all 12 chickens once. On desktop, use W/S to move forward and back, A/D to strafe, the mouse to look, left click to pet, Shift to sprint and Esc to pause. Get close to an unpetted chicken and face it before clicking. On touchscreens, use the joystick, swipe to look and tap PET when you are in range. Play the farm game.

Aim and typing practice

Aim Trainer offers flick shots, target switching, moving targets and tracking. Its five-minute routine runs four 75-second drills. Keep your settings consistent when comparing sessions. This browser trainer does not reproduce another game's raw input or field of view.

In Typing Speed, type the displayed passage and watch both speed and accuracy. Read the game's scoring explanation before comparing results with another typing site; a change in scoring rules can change the result.

Short puzzles and reaction practice

Daily Word gives you six guesses; Word Scramble asks you to rearrange letters. In Reaction Time, wait for green before pressing. Input and display delays affect reaction measurements, so use the same device to compare your own attempts.

If something does not work

  • A large file fails: check the tool's format and size limits, close memory-heavy tabs, and try a smaller non-sensitive example.
  • A download seems missing: check your browser's Downloads list or your phone's Files app. Open and inspect the exported file before sharing it.
  • A game is slow: pause it, close other demanding tabs, or try a smaller window. Three-dimensional games depend on device graphics support.
  • A saved result is gone: private browsing, cleared site data or a different browser can mean a different local record. Account sync covers supported game records, not every tool.

See the FAQ for storage and privacy details. To report a problem, email countmywords.com@gmail.com with the page, browser, expected result and a small non-sensitive example.
