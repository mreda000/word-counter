How to Use ChickenTask

From 3D farm adventures to quick puzzles and writing tools, here's how to get started with everything on the site.

Start playing in three steps

1. Browse the Games & Tools Hub.

Open Games & Tools and pick an experience.

2. Follow the controls on the game page.

Some games use the mouse and keyboard, while others offer touchscreen controls or mobile-friendly inputs.

3. Practice and beat your best.

Several games save records in your current browser. Optionally sign in with Google or email to sync supported game records. You can keep playing as a guest.

🐔 Pet the Chicken

Explore the 3D farm as a first-person farmer. Find 12 roaming chickens, get close, face them, and pet each one once to finish.

Desktop

W — forward · S — backward · A/D — strafe left/right · Mouse — look · Left click — pet · Shift — sprint · Esc — pause

iPhone, Android & tablets

Drag the on-screen joystick to move, swipe across the farm to look, press the PET button near a chicken, and use the pause button when needed. The game uses WebGL, so performance can vary by device.

🎯 Aim Trainer

Choose flick shots, target switching, moving targets or smooth tracking. Adjust target size, movement speed, color, round duration and crosshair; or select a preset.

Quick warm-up:

Choose the five-minute routine. It automatically runs four 75-second drills and shows a performance breakdown. Click or tap targets for most drills; tracking mode measures time your pointer stays over moving targets.

Mouse practice is recommended. Browser cursor movement doesn't exactly reproduce raw input, field of view or sensitivity in Valorant or CS2.

📝 Word Counter & Text Analyzer

The original text tool now lives on its own page, linked from Games & Tools—not the homepage.

1. Enter text.

Type or paste text to instantly see word, character, number, sentence and paragraph counts.

2. Review estimates.

Reading time, speaking time and an approximate English reading grade update along with your text.

3. Copy, clear or fetch.

Copy your results, clear the editor, or enter a webpage URL and select Fetch & Analyze. Many external sites restrict browser fetching (CORS), so you may need to paste text manually.
