Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

ChickenTask offers browser games, practice challenges and useful tools. This policy describes how we handle information when you use the site.

Game progress and browser storage

When playing as a guest, some personal best scores, game settings and puzzle progress are stored in your browser's local storage. Clearing your browser's site data may erase these records. The Word Counter analyzes text you type or paste locally in your browser. Optional URL fetching sends a browser request to the source website, which may block it.

Player accounts and cloud saving

If account sign-in is enabled and you choose to register, ChickenTask uses Google Firebase Authentication for Google sign-in or email/password accounts. Firebase processes the account identifiers and authentication information needed to sign you in. Supported game scores, records and settings may be saved to Google Cloud Firestore under your user ID so they can be accessed while signed in. ChickenTask's website code does not store your account password as a game record. Guest play remains available without signing in.

Everyday tools

Image to Text and PDF Merger process selected files on your device. These tools do not upload file contents or recognized text. Recognition software and language data load from this site and may be cached by your browser. Sending extracted text to Word Counter uses session storage, which is cleared after transfer. Focus Timer saves task descriptions and duration preferences in local storage; use Clear tasks or clear site data to remove them. These tools do not sync files, text or tasks to a player account. Ordinary site services may still make their own network requests.

Optional analytics

Google Analytics 4 is off by default. The analytics library is downloaded only after you choose Allow analytics in the footer's Privacy choices panel. If enabled, Google receives usage information, including pages visited, browser and device details, and interaction information. We do not send text entered in tools, selected files, task descriptions or game input to Analytics. Google Signals and advertising personalization features in our analytics configuration are disabled.

Your analytics choice is stored on this device for up to 180 days. You can turn analytics off at any time in Privacy choices; this disables further analytics collection and clears accessible first-party Google Analytics cookies. Turning it off does not erase information already received by Google. Clearing browser storage removes the saved choice and leaves optional analytics off.

Advertising and regional privacy choices

ChickenTask uses Google AdSense. Google and participating advertising providers may receive technical information such as your IP address, page URL, device and browser details, and advertising interactions. They may use cookies or similar storage according to the consent choices and rules that apply to your visit. Read how Google uses information from sites that use its services and manage Google account advertising preferences in My Ad Center.

Where a regional Google consent message is available, use it to accept, decline or manage advertising choices. The Privacy choices panel also provides a way to reopen the European advertising message when Google's consent service is available. Where a US state opt-out message applies, use Google's Do not sell or share link. Optional analytics is a separate choice; allowing analytics does not grant advertising consent. Games and tools remain available when optional analytics is off or advertising consent is declined.

Browser storage also remembers features you request, including theme preferences, game progress and tool settings. The analytics choice panel does not clear those records. You can remove them using the relevant tool's controls or your browser's site-data settings.

Service providers

Our website is hosted through Netlify, and we use third-party services for games and website functionality. The 3D farm game currently loads the Three.js library from a CDN. Google Firebase provides optional account authentication and cloud data storage. Each provider may process technical information needed to deliver its service.

Your choices

You can play supported games without creating an account. You may clear locally saved game data through your browser settings. For questions about accessing or deleting account data, contact countmywords.com@gmail.com. We may need to verify account ownership before processing a request. We may update this policy when features or practices change.
