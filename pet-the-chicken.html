Pet the Chicken

You're a farmer on a mission. Explore the farm, spot 12 wandering chickens, and pet them all. Look around with your mouse and click to pet when you're close!

On phones and tablets: drag the joystick to move, swipe the game to look around, and tap PET near a chicken. On desktop: WASD + mouse. Best time saves on this device.

Explore the farm and find all 12 chickens

This first-person game is a search around a small 3D farm. Enter the farm, walk toward a chicken and face it, then pet it. Each chicken counts once. A heart confirms a successful interaction and the counter shows how many you have found. The round finishes when all 12 have been petted.

Desktop and touch controls

On desktop, use W, A, S and D to move, move the mouse to look, left-click to pet, and hold Shift to sprint. Entering the farm captures the pointer so you can turn without running out of screen space. Press Esc to release the cursor and pause. On a phone or tablet, use the on-screen joystick to move, swipe the game view to look and use PET when close to a chicken.

A route for your first search

  1. Walk around nearby open ground and learn how close you need to be for an interaction.
  2. Use the barn and fence as landmarks so you do not keep searching the same area.
  3. Check behind scenery, then work around the farm's outer areas.
  4. Watch for hearts and the counter to distinguish found chickens from those still needed.

Take a slow exploration round before trying for a time. Sprinting can cover ground quickly, but makes it easier to pass a chicken or lose track of your route. The chickens wander, so a route is a search strategy rather than a fixed list of coordinates.

Records and loading problems

The fastest completed time is stored on this device. Refreshing starts a new visit rather than resuming an unfinished search. If petting does nothing, move closer and face the chicken. If the 3D scene does not appear, check that your browser supports WebGL and that its graphics features and the library download are available. Older devices may perform better with other tabs closed. You can also choose a lighter game such as Daily Word or Word Scramble.
