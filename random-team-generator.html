How to use Random Team Generator

Paste your player list, choose how many teams you need, and generate. Players are shuffled and distributed evenly. If you enable captains, the first randomly assigned player on each team gets the captain label.

Are teams balanced by skill?

Teams are balanced by player count, not skill. No ratings or personal information are needed. Generate again for another random arrangement.
