How to use QR Code Generator

Choose text/link or Wi-Fi, enter the details, and create your QR code. Download PNG for everyday sharing or SVG for sharp printing at any size. These are static codes: ChickenTask does not add a tracking redirect or require a subscription.

Will my QR code expire?

The encoded data does not expire. A linked website can still move or go offline, and a Wi-Fi password can change. Test scans on your actual target devices before distributing the code.
