Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about playing, practicing and using ChickenTask's free tools.

Getting started

What is ChickenTask?

ChickenTask is a website with free browser games, practice challenges and helpful tools. You can explore a farm in Pet the Chicken, train your aim, test your typing, play word puzzles and use tools for PDFs, image-to-text recognition, calculations and writing.

Is ChickenTask free?

The current games and tools are free to use. You may see advertising that helps support the site.

Do I need to make an account or install anything?

No account or download is required to play. You can optionally sign in with Google or email to save supported game records to your account.

Can I use ChickenTask on an iPhone, Android device or tablet?

Yes, the website includes responsive layouts and mobile-friendly controls where practical. Pet the Chicken offers an on-screen joystick, swipe-to-look and PET button. Some challenges are easier with a mouse or keyboard.

Games and challenges

How do I play Pet the Chicken?

Find and pet all 12 chickens scattered around the 3D farm. Desktop: use W/S to move forward/backward, A/D to strafe, mouse to look, left click to pet, Shift to sprint and Esc to pause. Mobile: use the joystick, swipe to look and tap PET near a chicken.

Why can't I pet a chicken?

Move closer to an unpetted chicken and face it. A petting prompt appears once you're in range. Each chicken can be petted once per round.

How does the five-minute Aim Trainer work?

It runs four automatic 75-second drills: flick shots, target switching, moving targets and tracking. You'll see results when the routine finishes.

Does Aim Trainer use my exact Valorant or CS2 settings?

No. Browser pointer behavior, FOV and sensitivity differ from games with raw mouse input. Think of it as general practice, not a calibrated game simulator.

What is WPM in the typing game?

WPM means words per minute. The game also reports accuracy to help you balance speed and mistakes.

How do Daily Word and Word Scramble work?

Daily Word offers a word puzzle with up to six guesses. Word Scramble asks you to rearrange letters to find the correct word. Both are available in Games.

Tools, saved progress and privacy

Where is the Word Counter?

Open Word Counter & Text Analyzer directly, or find it in Tools. The page includes editable examples and an explanation of how the counts and estimates work.

What can the Word Counter measure?

Words, characters, numbers, sentences, paragraphs, reading time, speaking time and an approximate English reading grade.

Why doesn't Fetch & Analyze work on every URL?

Many websites block browser-based requests due to CORS restrictions. If fetching fails, copy the text and paste it into the Word Counter.

Are my scores saved on other devices?

Some games save best scores and preferences in your browser. If you sign in, supported records also sync to your account. Guest records stay on this browser and may be lost if you clear site data or use private mode. On shared devices, local records can be associated with whichever account signs in.

What happens to my text and other data?

Typed text is analyzed in your browser. Optional URL fetching sends a browser request to the source website. For details about cookies, analytics and advertising, review the Privacy Policy.

Why are there advertisements?

ChickenTask may use advertising to help keep its games and tools available free of charge.

Files, calculations and focus

Are my images, PDFs and extracted text uploaded?

The image, PDF and text tools process the selected content in your browser. They do not upload those files or save them to a ChickenTask account. The website still loads resources and uses hosting, advertising and optional account services, which can receive technical information. Read the Privacy Policy for the distinction.

Why does Image to Text make mistakes?

Recognition is configured for printed English. Small, blurry, tilted or handwritten text can be misread. Check the result against the image before copying, downloading or sending it to Word Counter, especially names and numbers. Use JPG, PNG or WebP within the displayed limits of 15 MB and 20 megapixels.

Does PDF Merger change my original files?

No. It creates a new download from the pages you choose. The current batch limits are 10 files, 50 MB in total and 100 pages. Password-protected PDFs are not supported. Forms, bookmarks and digital signatures may not be retained or remain valid in the output, so keep your originals and inspect the download.

Why is my compressed image different?

Compression can discard image detail, and resizing reduces dimensions. This tool exports JPEG or WebP and flattens transparent areas to white. Check the preview, file size and dimensions against what you need. Preserve the original if you need transparency or original quality.

Will my QR code expire or track scans?

The generator makes a static code containing the information you enter; ChickenTask does not add a tracking redirect or an expiry date. A linked website can still change or become unavailable, and that website may collect its own traffic data. Scan the exported code to verify the exact destination before sharing it.

Why do two percentage calculations give different answers?

The base matters. A decrease from 80 to 60 is 25% of 80; an increase from 60 back to 80 is about 33.33% of 60. Use the appropriate mode in Percentage Calculator and check the formula shown with your result.

Will the focus timer run if I close the page?

No. Keep the page open. The timer uses a finish time to catch up after a background pause, but a browser can suspend the tab and delay the completion display. Preferences and tasks can be saved locally; the active countdown is not a background notification service or an account-synced timer.

Where are my tasks, favorite names and saved wheels stored?

These features use this browser's local storage when it is available. They do not follow you to a different device through game-account sign-in. Clear tasks with the tool's control or remove site data through your browser to clear local records. A wheel share link contains its entries, so share it only with the intended people.

Can I control optional analytics and advertising choices?

Open Privacy choices in the footer to allow or turn off optional analytics. Analytics is off by default. Advertising consent is separate: use Google's regional consent message and available privacy links when they are shown. You can keep using the tools without enabling optional analytics.
