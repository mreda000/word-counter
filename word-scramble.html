Word Scramble Game

Unscramble words in a free 60-second word game. Earn points for correct answers and use hints when you get stuck.

How to play Word Scramble Game

Inspect the scrambled letters, then submit your first answer to start the timer. Correct answers earn 10 points; hints cost 2 points. Play again to improve your score.

Make the most of your 60 seconds

Rearrange every displayed letter to recover the selected word, type your answer, then press Enter or Submit. The timer starts on your first submission, including an incorrect one. You can inspect the opening letters before starting. Correct answers earn 10 points and immediately display another scramble; incorrect answers leave the same letters on screen.

Hints and scoring

A hint reveals the answer's first two letters and its length. Each press costs 2 points, with the score never going below zero. For example, four solved words and one hint gives 38 points. Repeated hint presses reveal the same clue and still cost points, so read the clue before pressing again. Choosing New round resets the timer and score.

A practical solving method

  1. Count the letters and separate vowels from consonants.
  2. Look for familiar pairs or endings, such as TH, ER or ING, when those letters are present.
  3. Try a candidate and check that every letter is used exactly once.
  4. If you are stuck, use one hint and place its first two letters before rebuilding the rest.

The game draws from a small set of words, so practice may help you recognize answers and words can repeat within a round. A higher score can reflect familiarity as well as faster solving. There is no permanent local high-score table for this game.

If an answer is rejected

Check for missing or repeated letters. Letter case does not matter and leading or trailing spaces are ignored, but internal spaces do not match a single-word answer. When time expires, answer and hint buttons are disabled until you start a new round. For a slower deduction puzzle, try Daily Word.
